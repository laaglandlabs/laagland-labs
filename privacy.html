LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy matters.

Pre-launch draft. This page is not a final privacy policy. LoonLoop is still in development, and we will update this page to match the actual released app, including its optional feedback system, before publication.

Our approach

Laagland Labs aims to build useful software with data minimization in mind. LoonLoop is designed as an offline-first budgeting app, with financial records stored locally on the user's device.

Optional feedback

We plan to offer optional bug reporting and suggestions. If implemented, submitted messages and any clearly disclosed diagnostic details may be processed to respond to feedback. The final policy will specify the exact service providers, data types, retention periods, and user rights.

Website

This static website has no account system, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies in this version. Hosting providers may still process ordinary server access logs. Contact links open your email application.
